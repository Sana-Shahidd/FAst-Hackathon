# Subah — your personal morning paper

Subah (صبح, "morning") gathers the articles that matter to **you** from across the web and
lays them out as one personal morning newspaper — readable on the web, downloadable as a
newspaper-style PDF, in **8 languages**, and smarter every day you read it.

A small team of AI agents builds each edition: the **Profiler** learns what you care about
during onboarding, the **Curator** picks today's stories, the **Editor** writes the front
page in your language, and the **Learner** improves tomorrow's paper from what you read and skip.
